CENTRAL GOVERNMENT SCHEMES - 85 BENCHMARK QUESTIONS & DIRECT ANSWERS
Reference Source: myScheme Portal (https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) & Official Scheme Guidelines

Q1. Who can apply for PM-KISAN?
A1. All landholding farmer families across India who have cultivable land registered in their names are eligible. Excluded from the scheme are institutional landholders, constitutional post holders, serving or retired government employees/officers, pensioners drawing Rs 10,000 or more per month, individuals who paid income tax in the last assessment year, and registered professionals (doctors, lawyers, engineers, chartered accountants, architects).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q2. Am I eligible for PMEGP?
A2. Any individual aged 18 years and above is eligible. There is no family income ceiling. To establish a manufacturing project costing above Rs 10 lakh or a service project costing above Rs 5 lakh, the applicant must have passed at least Class 8. Only new units are eligible for initial funding. Self-Help Groups (SHGs), registered cooperative societies, and charitable trusts are also eligible.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q3. What documents do I need for PMAY-Gramin?
A3. 
- Aadhaar card of applicant and all adult family members
- Bank account details (passbook copy linked with Aadhaar)
- MGNREGA Job Card number
- Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM) registration number (for the Rs 12,000 toilet incentive)
- Land ownership document / revenue record or affidavit of landlessness/no pucca house
- Signed Aadhaar consent form.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q4. Compare PMJJBY and PMSBY.
A4. 
- Type of Risk Cover: PMJJBY covers death due to any reason (life insurance); PMSBY covers accidental death and permanent/partial disability only.
- Coverage Limit: Both provide Rs 2,00,000 (PMSBY provides Rs 1,00,000 for permanent partial disability).
- Eligible Age Bracket: PMJJBY is 18 to 50 years (life cover extends up to 55 years); PMSBY is 18 to 70 years.
- Annual Premium: PMJJBY costs Rs 436 per annum; PMSBY costs Rs 20 per annum.
- Payment Mode: Auto-debited annually from the subscriber's bank or post office savings account.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q5. I'm a farmer in Telangana with 2 hectares of agricultural land, which central government schemes can I benefit from?
A5. As a small/marginal farmer with 2 hectares (~4.94 acres):
- PM-KISAN: Rs 6,000 annual income support in 3 equal installments of Rs 2,000.
- PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY): Subsidized crop insurance (1.5% premium for Rabi, 2% for Kharif, 5% for commercial/horticultural crops).
- Kisan Credit Card (KCC): Short-term crop loans up to Rs 3 lakh at an effective 4% interest rate (with 2% subvention and 3% prompt repayment incentive).
- PM Kisan Maan-Dhan Yojana (PM-KMDY): Contributory pension of Rs 3,000 per month after age 60 (entry age 18-40, holding up to 2 hectares).
- PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop): Up to 55% capital subsidy for drip/sprinkler irrigation systems.
- Soil Health Card Scheme: Periodic free soil nutrient testing and customized fertilizer advisory.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q6. What is the maximum loan limit and subsidy available under the PMMY Mudra Tarun category?
A6. 
- Maximum Loan Limit: Above Rs 5,00,000 and up to Rs 10,00,000 (under Tarun Plus, this limit is extended up to Rs 20,00,000 for borrowers who have successfully repaid previous Tarun loans).
- Subsidy: Mudra loans do not offer direct cash or capital subsidies; they offer collateral-free credit with credit guarantee backing under CGFMU (Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units).

Q7. What are the key differences between Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY and the Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS)?
A7. 
- Beneficiary Base: PM-JAY targets the bottom 40% vulnerable and poor families identified by SECC 2011 / state food security registries, plus all senior citizens aged 70+ regardless of income. CGHS caters to serving and retired Central Government civil employees, pensioners, MPs, and their dependent family members.
- Benefit Scope: PM-JAY provides up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary inpatient hospitalization. CGHS provides comprehensive primary, outpatient (OPD) dispensaries, generic medicines, investigations, and secondary/tertiary inpatient treatments.
- Cost: PM-JAY is 100% government-funded and free for beneficiaries. CGHS requires monthly payroll deductions from employees and subscription fees from pensioners.

Q9. I am a 24-year-old unemployed graduate from an SC background looking to start a manufacturing unit. Which schemes provide financial backing?
A9. 
- Stand-Up India: Facilitates bank loans between Rs 10 lakh and Rs 1 crore for greenfield manufacturing enterprises specifically reserved for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs.
- PMEGP (Special Category): Project loans up to Rs 50 lakh for manufacturing with a 35% margin money subsidy in rural areas (25% in urban areas), requiring only a 5% personal contribution.
- Venture Capital Fund for Scheduled Castes (VCF-SC): Concessional equity/debt financing up to Rs 15 crore for SC-promoted enterprises.
- PM Mudra Yojana: Collateral-free loans up to Rs 10-20 lakh under Kishore/Tarun tiers.

Q10. Is an income tax payee eligible to receive the Rs 6,00,000 annual benefit under PM-KISAN?
A10. No. Any individual who paid income tax in the last assessment year, or whose spouse/minor child is an income tax payee, is strictly excluded from PM-KISAN.

Q11. Who can apply for the PM Vishwakarma scheme, and what trades are covered under it?
A11. 
- Eligibility: Artisans and craftspersons aged 18+ working with their hands and traditional tools in the unorganized sector on self-employment basis (one member per family, with no credit availed under PMEGP/Mudra/PM SVANidhi in the previous 5 years).
- 18 Covered Trades: Carpenter (Suthar), Boat Maker, Armourer, Blacksmith (Lohar), Hammer and Tool Kit Maker, Locksmith, Sculptor (Moortikar/Stone carver), Stone Breaker, Goldsmith (Sonar), Potter (Kumhaar), Cobbler (Charmakar)/Shoemaker, Mason (Rajmistri), Basket/Mat/Broom Maker/Coir Weaver, Doll & Toy Maker (Traditional), Barber (Naai), Garland Maker (Malakaar), Washerman (Dhobi), Tailor (Darzi), and Fishing Net Maker.

Q12. What is the subsidy percentage under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana for a 2 kW residential rooftop solar system?
A12. Under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, residential rooftop solar subsidies are structured as:
- 1 kW capacity: Rs 30,000
- 2 kW capacity: Rs 60,000 (providing roughly up to 60% of the benchmark system cost for the first 2 kW). For systems above 2 kW up to 3 kW, an additional Rs 18,000 is given, capping the maximum subsidy at Rs 78,000.

Q13. What is the entry age and monthly contribution required to receive a Rs 3,000 monthly pension under Atal Pension Yojana (APY)?
A13. 
- Entry Age: 18 to 40 years.
- Monthly Contribution: Varies according to joining age. If joining at age 18, the monthly contribution is Rs 126; at age 25 it is Rs 226; at age 30 it is Rs 347; at age 35 it is Rs 543; and at age 40 it is Rs 898 per month until reaching age 60.

Q14. Can a street vendor get a collateral-free working capital loan under PM SVANidhi, and what is the interest subsidy?
A14. Yes. Urban street vendors holding a Vending Certificate / Letter of Recommendation can access graduated collateral-free loans:
- 1st Tranche: Up to Rs 10,000 (1-year repayment tenure)
- 2nd Tranche: Up to Rs 20,000 (on timely repayment of 1st tranche)
- 3rd Tranche: Up to Rs 50,000.
- Interest Subsidy: 7% per annum credited directly to the borrower's bank account every quarter on timely repayment, alongside digital cashback up to Rs 1,200 per year.

Q15. What are the eligibility criteria and maximum project cost allowed under PMEGP for manufacturing and service sectors?

- Maximum Project Cost:
  - Manufacturing Sector: Rs 50,00,000 (up to Rs 1 Crore for a second loan for enterprise upgradation).
  - Service / Business Sector: Rs 20,00,000 (up to Rs 25 Lakh for a second loan for enterprise upgradation).

Q16. Compare Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U) and Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) in terms of assistance amount and target beneficiaries.
A16. 
- Target Beneficiaries: PMAY-G targets rural households living in kutcha/dilapidated houses or without shelter identified via SECC 2011 and Awaas+ list. PMAY-U (PMAY-U 2.0) targets urban poor in EWS, LIG, and MIG categories who do not own a pucca house in India.
- Assistance Amount:
  - PMAY-G: Direct grant of Rs 1,20,000 in plain areas and Rs 1,30,000 in hilly/difficult/northeastern states/IAP districts, plus 90/95 days of unskilled wages under MGNREGA (~Rs 25,000) and Rs 12,000 for toilet under SBM-G.
  - PMAY-U: Up to Rs 1.5 - 2.5 lakh direct assistance for Beneficiary-Led Construction (BLC), or interest subsidies (up to Rs 1.80 - 2.67 lakh) on home loans under the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme / Interest Subsidy Scheme.

Q17. I am an artisan working in traditional pottery. Can I get collateral-free credit and toolkit incentives from the central government?
A17. Yes, under the PM Vishwakarma Scheme (Potter/Kumhaar is one of the 18 covered trades):
- Toolkit Incentive: E-voucher / digital grant of Rs 15,000.

- Collateral-Free Enterprise Credit: Tranche 1 up to Rs 1,00,000 (18-month tenure) and Tranche 2 up to Rs 2,00,000 (30-month tenure) at a concessional interest rate of 5% (with an 8% subvention borne by the government).

Q18. What is the premium rate and coverage limit under Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)?
A18. 
- Premium Rate: Rs 20 per year per subscriber (auto-debited in a single installment from the linked bank/post office account before 1st June annually).
- Coverage Limit: Rs 2,00,000 for accidental death or total permanent disability (loss of both eyes, both hands, or both feet); Rs 1,00,000 for permanent partial disability (loss of sight of one eye or loss of use of one hand/foot).

- Benefits: 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN) card valid pan-India; seamless portability of social security benefits; direct access to PMSBY accidental insurance cover during enrollment drives; automated targeting for DBT cash assistance during emergencies/disasters; and integrated access to employment and skilling portals (NCS, ASEEM).

Q20. Who is eligible to receive benefits under Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) for first and second children?
A20. 
- Eligibility: Pregnant women and lactating mothers aged 19+ belonging to socially or economically vulnerable groups (e.g., family income < Rs 8 lakh/year, SC/ST, BPL/E-Shram/MGNREGA/PM-JAY cardholders, Divyangjan). Regular Central/State Govt and PSU employees are excluded.
- First Child: Rs 5,000 paid in 2 installments (Rs 3,000 at early ANC registration; Rs 2,000 after child birth and primary vaccination).
- Second Child: Rs 6,000 in a single installment after birth and vaccination, provided the second child is a girl child.

Q21. What is the difference between PM-SYM (Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-dhan) and Atal Pension Yojana (APY)?
A21. 
- Beneficiary Profile: PM-SYM is strictly for unorganized workers aged 18-40 earning up to Rs 15,000/month who are not members of EPFO, ESIC, or NPS and not income tax payers. APY is open to any Indian citizen aged 18-40 holding a savings bank account (since October 2022, income tax payers are barred).
- Pension Choice: PM-SYM has a single fixed pension of Rs 3,000/month after age 60. APY offers 5 distinct monthly pension slabs: Rs 1,000, Rs 2,000, Rs 3,000, Rs 4,000, or Rs 5,000.
- Contribution Sharing: In PM-SYM, the Central Government matches 50% of the worker's monthly contribution for life. In APY, the subscriber pays the full actuarial contribution table.

Q22. I am a small farmer whose paddy crop was damaged due to heavy unseasonal rains. How can I claim compensation under PMFBY?
A22. 
- Step 1: Intimate the crop loss within 72 hours of the calamity through the Crop Insurance App, toll-free number 14447, bank branch, or local agricultural officer.
- Step 2: Provide policy number, crop name, affected acreage, and date of damage.
- Step 3: A joint survey is conducted by an insurance company representative and state agriculture official within 7-10 days to quantify loss.
- Step 4: The claim amount is assessed and credited directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account.

Q26. What financial assistance does Nikshay Poshan Yojana provide to TB patients, and is it transferred via DBT?
A26. 
- Assistance: Rs 500 per month (revised upwards to Rs 1,000/month under enhanced nutritional support guidelines) for the full duration of anti-TB treatment.
- DBT Transfer: Yes, 100% of the incentive is transferred directly into the patient's Aadhaar-seeded bank account through the centralized Ni-kshay platform.

Q27. Compare Kisan Credit Card (KCC) and Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) in terms of purpose and interest subvention.
A27. 
- Purpose: KCC funds short-term cultivation expenses, post-harvest costs, and maintenance of farm assets/allied activities (dairy, poultry, fisheries). AIF finances medium-to-long term post-harvest management infrastructure and community farming assets (cold storage, warehouses, silos, grading/sorting packs).
- Loan Term: KCC is revolving working capital valid for 5 years with annual review; AIF offers term loans up to 10 years (with moratorium up to 2 years).
- Interest Subvention: KCC provides 2% interest subvention and 3% prompt repayment incentive on crop loans up to Rs 3 lakh (effective 4% p.a.). AIF provides 3% per annum interest subvention on capital loans up to Rs 2 crore for up to 7 years, along with CGTMSE fee coverage.

Q28. What are the eligibility criteria for students applying for the Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students (CSSS)?
A28. 
- Educational Merit: Must score above the 80th percentile of successful candidates in the respective stream in the Class 12 (10+2) board examination.
- Enrolment: Enrolled in regular (full-time, non-distance) degree/professional courses at recognized colleges/universities approved by UGC/AICTE.
- Income Limit: Total annual parental/family income must not exceed Rs 4,50,000.
- Exclusivity: Beneficiary must not be availing of any other state or central government scholarship.

Q29. I am a rural woman wanting to form a Self-Help Group (SHG) to start a micro-enterprise. How does DAY-NRLM assist us?
A29. DAY-NRLM provides:
- Revolving Fund (RF): Rs 20,000 to Rs 30,000 per SHG to initiate internal lending among members.
- Community Investment Support Fund (CIF): Up to Rs 1.5 lakh per SHG routed through Village Organizations for expanding livelihood activities.
- Collateral-Free Bank Credit: Bank linkage loans without collateral up to Rs 10 lakh (and up to Rs 20 lakh for standard SHGs).
- Interest Subvention: Concessional interest rate of 7% on loans up to Rs 3 lakh, with an additional 3% subvention for prompt repayment (effective interest 4%).
- Capacity building, enterprise training, and market access through SARAS Melas and GeM/e-commerce.

Q31. What is the eligibility condition for availing LPG connections under Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY / Ujjwala 2.0)?

Q33. Am I eligible for PM-JANMAN if I belong to a Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group (PVTG)? What components are included?
A33. Yes. Members of the 75 notified PVTG communities residing across 18 States and Union Territories are eligible.
- Included Components: Pucca housing (enhanced PMAY-G grant of Rs 2.39 lakh per house), piped drinking water to every household, all-weather village road connectivity, grid and off-grid solar electrification, mobile medical units, community health centres, Anganwadi nutrition hubs, multi-purpose community centres, 4G mobile towers, and vocational training/Van Dhan Vikas Kendras.

Q34. What is the difference between PMMY (Mudra) and Stand-Up India schemes?
A34. 
- Beneficiary Focus: Mudra is universal and accessible to all micro-enterprises and non-corporate small business owners; Stand-Up India is exclusively earmarked for SC, ST, and Women entrepreneurs.
- Quantum of Loan: Mudra caters to smaller loans from Rs 10,000 up to Rs 10 Lakh (extended to Rs 20 Lakh under Tarun Plus); Stand-Up India funds larger projects between Rs 10 Lakh and Rs 1 Crore.
- Unit Stage: Mudra finances both new and existing businesses for expansion; Stand-Up India strictly finances greenfield (newly established) enterprises.

- Qualification: Depends entirely on the specific Job Role / National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) level (ranging from 5th/8th pass to 10th/12th, ITI, or graduates).

Q36. I am a handloom weaver in Varanasi. Which central schemes can provide me with subsidized yarn and modern loom upgrades?

Q37. How can a person apply for an assistive device under the ADIP Scheme for Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan)?
A37. 
- Eligibility: Person with benchmark disability of 40% or more, possessing a UDID card or disability certificate, with monthly income not exceeding Rs 30,000 (100% subsidy for income up to Rs 22,500/month; 50% subsidy for income between Rs 22,501 and Rs 30,000).

Q38. What are the criteria to be covered under the Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) category under the National Food Security Act (NFSA)?

Q39. Does PM-JAY cover pre-existing diseases from day one of hospitalization?
A39. Yes. Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY covers all pre-existing medical conditions and chronic illnesses from day one of card activation, with zero waiting periods, zero exclusions, and completely cashless treatment up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year.

Q40. What is the credit guarantee coverage provided under CGTMSE for micro and small enterprises?
A40. 
- Guarantee Extent: 75% to 85% of the default risk (up to 85% for micro-enterprises borrowing up to Rs 5 lakh, women-owned units, SC/ST entrepreneurs, and ZED-certified units; 75% for standard MSE loans).
- Loan Limit: Covers collateral-free credit facilities up to Rs 5.00 Crore per eligible borrower unit.

Q41. Compare PM-KISAN with Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maan-Dhan Yojana (PM-KMDY).
A41. 
- Scheme Goal: PM-KISAN provides immediate annual income support; PM-KMDY provides old-age social security pension after age 60.
- Financial Payout: PM-KISAN pays Rs 6,000 per year (3 installments of Rs 2,000); PM-KMDY pays an assured lifelong pension of Rs 3,000 per month.
- Contribution Model: PM-KISAN is 100% non-contributory funded by the Central Govt; PM-KMDY requires voluntary monthly contributions of Rs 55 to Rs 200 (matched 1:1 by Central Govt).
- Landholding Requirement: PM-KISAN covers all cultivable landholding farmer families; PM-KMDY is strictly limited to small and marginal farmers owning up to 2 hectares of cultivable land.

Q44. Who is eligible to apply for Prime Minister's Research Fellowship (PMRF), and what is the monthly stipend?

Q45. What are the subsidy provisions for installing solar agriculture pumps under PM-KUSUM Component-B?
A45.

Q46. Can government pensioners or their spouses avail benefits under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY?

However, under the expanded Ayushman Bharat policy, all senior citizens aged 70 years and above are eligible for a distinct top-up cover of Rs 5 lakh per year, but must choose between PM-JAY and their existing statutory healthcare benefit (CGHS/ECHS).

Q48. What is the difference between National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) pensions and PM-SYM?
A48. 
- Scheme Nature: NSAP (such as IGNOAPS) is a 100% non-contributory welfare safety net funded by government for destitute BPL citizens; PM-SYM is a contributory pension fund where the beneficiary and central government contribute 50:50 monthly.
- Eligibility Group: NSAP targets destitute elderly individuals (aged 60+), widows, and persons with severe disabilities living below poverty line. PM-SYM targets active unorganized workers aged 18-40 earning up to Rs 15,000/month.
- Pension Quantum: NSAP provides Rs 200–500 per month basic central assistance (augmented by state top-ups); PM-SYM guarantees an assured fixed pension of Rs 3,000 per month post age 60.

Q49. I am a fisherman wanting to buy an insulated ice box and a motorized fishing boat. Which components of PMMSY support this?
A49. Under Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY):
- Beneficiary-oriented sub-schemes finance the acquisition of motorized/traditional fishing craft and post-harvest quality infrastructure (insulated ice boxes, three-wheeler/two-wheeler fish transport units with ice boxes).
- Subsidy Assistance: Up to 40% of the project unit cost for General category beneficiaries and up to 60% of the project unit cost for SC, ST, and Women beneficiaries.

Q50. What is the interest subsidy under the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS) component of PMAY-U?
A50. 
- Under original CLSS: EWS/LIG borrowers (annual household income up to Rs 3 lakh and Rs 6 lakh) received a 6.5% interest subsidy on housing loans up to Rs 6,00,000 for a tenure of up to 20 years (total upfront NPV benefit ~Rs 2.67 lakh).
- Under PMAY-U 2.0 (Interest Subsidy Scheme): EWS/LIG/MIG families purchasing or constructing their first pucca home receive an interest subsidy of 4.0% on home loans up to Rs 25 lakh (for house values up to Rs 35 lakh), disbursed in 5 annual installments up to a total subsidy of Rs 1.80 lakh.

Q51. Who is eligible for the SMILE scheme (Support for Marginalised Individuals for Livelihood and Enterprise)?
A51. The SMILE scheme covers two target groups:

Q52. What are the eligibility guidelines for availing an education loan subsidy under the Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS) scheme?

Q53. Compare Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY) and PMKVY.
A53. 
- Target Demographic: DDU-GKY strictly focuses on poor rural youth aged 15-35 (up to 45 years for SC/ST/Women/PWD); PMKVY targets all urban and rural youth, school/college dropouts, and informal laborers.
- Placement Mandate: DDU-GKY is a placement-benchmarked scheme requiring project implementation agencies to place at least 70% of trained youth in formal wage employment with minimum defined salaries; PMKVY balances wage placement with self-employment, short-term courses, and Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL).
- Duration and Setup: DDU-GKY emphasizes longer-duration, residential, intensive skill programs; PMKVY mainly operates short-term modular skill certification (STT).

Q74. Compare Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) Scheme and Gold Monetization Scheme (GMS).
A74. 
- Asset Nature: SGB is a government debt security denominated in grams of gold issued by the RBI (paper/demat gold investment without holding physical gold). GMS is a bank deposit scheme where citizens deposit idle physical jewellery or gold coins into a bank account.
- Yield & Return: SGB pays a guaranteed semi-annual interest of 2.50% per annum on the initial issue price plus capital appreciation linked to market gold price. GMS pays interest (0.50% to 2.50% p.a. depending on short/medium/long term deposit) and returns physical gold or cash equivalent at maturity.
- Taxation: SGB capital gains on redemption at maturity (8 years) are 100% tax-free for individuals. GMS capital gains and interest earnings are also exempt from income tax and capital gains tax.

Q78. What is the difference between PM-JAY and Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana (RSBY)?
